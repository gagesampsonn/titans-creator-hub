Writing Your Prompt

• Be Specific & Visual

Describe exactly what you want: a clear location (e.g., "in the car," "at the gym"), a character (e.g., "Gen Z woman," "middle-aged dad"), or a mood (e.g., "hyped," "nostalgic"). The more concrete the scene (lighting, props, ambient sounds), the more realistic the AI output will be.

• Choose Avatar & Setting

Pick an avatar type (age, gender, background) and a setting (car selfie, bedroom, podcast mic). Matching those selections to your idea (e.g., "Gen Z rant in her car") helps the AI nail the right tone.

• Set Emotional Energy

Decide how intense or calm you want the clip: "excited/urgent," "intimate/honest," or "casual/chill." This guides the AI in building matching vocal cadence, breathing, and gestures.

• Frame Around One Focus

Keep your prompt centered on one clear idea (a rant about an ex, a pre-workout hype, a testimonial about ashwagandha). This ensures the script flows naturally without jumping between topics.

• Ask for One Continuous Take

Request "one continuous shot—no cuts." That makes the final video feel raw and authentic.

• Avoid Brand/Logo Overload

If you need a product mentioned, let it appear organically (e.g., "ripping open the ashwagandha bottle"). Avoid "show me a logo" or "zoom on brand name"—we want it to feel like a real person talking.
